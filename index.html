How it works

Three steps to a sealed home.

A real process, in order — because skipping the inspection or the seal is why pests come back.

STEP 01

Inspect & identify

A certified technician walks your property, finds the species and the source — entry points, moisture, nesting — and writes the plan around your home, not a script.

STEP 02

Treat & spray

We apply targeted, odor-free treatments where pests actually live and travel. Every product we use is pet- and family-safe — precisely applied, not fog-the-house overkill.

STEP 03

Seal & guard

We close the entry points and set up a recurring barrier — the LimeLock. If anything gets through between visits, we come back and re-treat free.
